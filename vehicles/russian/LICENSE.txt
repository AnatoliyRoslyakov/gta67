Pack of extremply low poly soviet vehicles in pixel art style.
Contains:
Vaz Classics: 2101 Yellow, grey, 2105 green, baklajan, police, 2106 blue, red.
Bobik (Uaz 469) : grey, khaki and police  modifications
Buhanka (Uaz 459) : grey, khaki, police, ER modifications
Bus liaz 677. Contains 1-frame opening door animation (UV coords shift {0.0, 0.5})
Tramway KTM5. Contains 1-frame opening door animation (UV coords shift {0.0, 0.5})

Adaptations for GTA67: metre scaling, separated replacement wheels, materials and Godot vehicle scenes.
The three VAZ liveries share the author's classic VAZ body mesh.

2026 model rebuild: the current Godot scenes use newly authored geometry and solid-color materials from tools/build_russian_models.gd. Earlier CC0 source geometry/textures have been replaced; established vehicle dimensions and mounting positions are retained. Original source attribution above is kept for provenance. No purchased POLYGON City geometry or textures are included.

UAZ-469 reference rebuild: original Blender geometry authored for GTA67 (tools/build_uaz469.py). Vehicle dimensions and reference URLs are recorded in the script and README. Reference photographs and purchased assets are not embedded or redistributed.
